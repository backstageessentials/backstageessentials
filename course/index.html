Who it is for

New stagehands in their first six months. House and corporate AV technicians. Student crews. Community theatre volunteers. CTE and community college programs that need a practical resource rather than a textbook.

What it does not do

It does not certify anyone. It is not a substitute for employer training, for a qualified person signing off on rigging or electrical work, or for the rules of the house you are standing in.
