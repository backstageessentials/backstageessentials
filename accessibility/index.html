This statement covers backstageessentials.com, all seven sheets, the error page, and this page.

Standard

The site aims to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Conformance

Based on the evaluation below, the site conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA, with one limitation described under Known limitations. That claim covers what was tested. It does not cover testing with assistive technology by the people who rely on it, which has not been done.

How it was evaluated

The site was tested on 17 November 2026 using axe-core 4.10.2, against the WCAG 2.0 Level A and AA and the WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA rule sets. All eight pages returned zero violations at three widths: a 1280 pixel laptop window, a 1920 pixel wide screen, and a 390 pixel phone.

Color contrast was measured separately. These pages sit on a paper texture made from images and gradients, and automated tools cannot resolve a background color behind text when the background is a texture. Each line of text was therefore measured against the pixels actually drawn behind it: 2,743 lines across the seven pages, at those three widths. Against the typical background behind each line, every line meets its minimum (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text). The lowest is 4.8:1.

Text resizing was tested at 200 percent, and every page was also tested at a 320 pixel width. No page produced horizontal scrolling.

What the site does

  • Semantic landmarks on every page: header, main, navigation, and footer
  • A skip link as the first focusable element
  • A visible focus indicator on every link
  • Text contrast at or above 4.5:1 against the typical paper color (see Known limitations)
  • Decorative notes and struck-out text hidden from assistive technology. What the notes say is also written in the regular text elsewhere on the site. The notes and desk props appear only on wide windows
  • Alternative text on every image
  • Heading levels in order, none skipped
  • Table headers marked with scope
  • Text that resizes to 200 percent without horizontal scrolling
  • A reduced motion rule for visitors who set that preference
  • No forms, no cookies, no analytics scripts, and no third party fonts

Known limitations

The site has not been tested with screen readers by people who use assistive technology daily. Automated testing finds structural problems. It does not establish that a page is usable.

The paper texture has small darker flecks. On under 1 percent of lines of text (16 of 2,743), a few of those flecks, or a table border, sit close behind part of the line, and the contrast there dips slightly below 4.5:1. Every line still meets its minimum against the typical background behind it.

My curriculum vitae is also offered as a PDF download. I have not assessed that file for accessibility. The complete CV is published as a web page on this site, and that version is what this statement covers.
