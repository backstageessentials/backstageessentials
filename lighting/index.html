Lighting design

Twenty four productions since 2016, around the Twin Cities and southeast Minnesota.

← index4of 5

My work includes productions for Theatre in the Round Players, Teatro Del Pueblo, TheatreX, Inver Hills Community College, Inver Grove Community Theatre, the Chameleon Theatre Circle, and the Phipps Center for the Arts. The productions range from black box performances to full-scale musicals.

I run ETC Element and Ion consoles and the Obsidian NX1-16. I draft in Vectorworks and store show files in Eos.
