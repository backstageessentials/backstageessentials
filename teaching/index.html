I teach audio and video production for live events and AV installation at Laurus College, and I lead a course on live event production at OLLI at the University of Minnesota. My day job is District AV Technology Coordinator at ISD 197, where I maintain the AV in eight buildings.

Teaching philosophy

Technical skills are learned by doing the work. My goal is to help students develop both the technical knowledge and professional habits needed to succeed in live event production and audiovisual technology.

The best learning happens when classroom concepts connect directly to workplace expectations. Whether the subject is audio systems, AV installation, stagecraft, or production operations, I try to anchor instruction in practical situations technicians are likely to encounter on the job.

My teaching is informed by work in live event production, AV technology administration, lighting design, workforce development, and higher education.
