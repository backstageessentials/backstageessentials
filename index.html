I am District AV Technology Coordinator at ISD 197. I teach audio and video production for live events at Laurus College.

I have designed lighting for twenty five productions since 2016, at Theatre in the Round Players, Teatro Del Pueblo, TheatreX, the Phipps Center for the Arts, and three other houses around the Twin Cities. I run ETC Element and Ion, draft in Vectorworks, and keep show files in Eos.
