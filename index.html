Light plot, Strega Nona, The Phipps Center for the Arts. Detail: two electrics. Lighting design by Bill Larsen.

I have designed lighting for twenty four productions since 2016, at Theatre in the Round Players, Teatro Del Pueblo, TheatreX, the Phipps Center for the Arts, and three other houses around the Twin Cities. I run ETC Element and Ion, draft in Vectorworks, and keep show files in Eos.

The rest of the week I am District AV Technology Coordinator at ISD 197, where the AV in eight buildings is mine. I teach audio and video production for live events at Laurus College.
