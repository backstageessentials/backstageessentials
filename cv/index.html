Summary

I work across live event production, audiovisual technology, technical education, and workforce development. My professional experience includes AV system administration, live event production, theatrical lighting design, college instruction, curriculum development, and educational publishing.

I teach audio and video production for live events, AV installation, and related technical subjects. I write and revise CTS certification exam items as an AVIXA Subject Matter Expert and develop educational resources for technicians entering the industry.

My book, Backstage Essentials: Skills for Stagehand Technicians in Live Event Production, will be published by Routledge on March 24, 2027.
