Backstage Essentials is a practical introduction to the work performed by stagehands and event technicians. Topics include production operations, safety, power distribution, audio, lighting, video, communication, troubleshooting, and professional expectations.

This book was written to help readers understand how live event work is actually performed. The emphasis is on practical knowledge, workplace expectations, and the skills needed to contribute effectively on a production crew.

Why I wrote it

For many new technicians, the hardest part of entering the industry is understanding what the job actually involves. Training resources often focus on theatre, individual technical disciplines, or equipment references.

Backstage Essentials was written as an introduction to the work itself. It brings together production operations, technical systems, crew expectations, and workplace practices in a format intended for students, entry-level technicians, and training programs.

The material reflects experience in live event production, audiovisual technology, technical education, and workforce development.

Who it is for

This book assumes no prior professional experience. It is intended for readers who are preparing for their first production call, participating in a student crew, volunteering in community theatre, or entering a live event production program.

  • New stagehands
  • Student production crews
  • Technical theatre students
  • Community theatre volunteers
  • Live event technicians
  • CTE programs
