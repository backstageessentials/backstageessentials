Backstage Essentials

Under Contract · Routledge 2026

A guide for new stagehand technicians in live event production. Load-in and load-out, audio, lighting, video, crew responsibilities, and safety.

Not a theory text and not a history text. It is a how-to guide for doing the job.

Readers

Who it is for

If you have never worked a call, this is the book. It assumes nothing.

This is not a stagecraft book. It is for the person who wants the job and has not worked a call yet.

This book is for
  • → New stagehands
  • → Student production crews
  • → Technical theatre students
  • → Community theatre volunteers
